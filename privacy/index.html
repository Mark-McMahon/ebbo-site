Ebbo

Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Ebbo (on the App Store as "Heart Rate Variability: Ebbo") is an iPhone app made by Mark McMahon, an independent developer ("we" and "us" below). This policy explains what Ebbo collects, why, who receives it and what you can do about it. If you have a question, email mmcmahon1102@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your Apple Health data stays on your iPhone. Ebbo reads it to work out your morning score. The readings, and anything Ebbo calculates from them, are not sent anywhere.
  • Ebbo has no account, no login and no server of its own.
  • To improve the app, Ebbo sends usage events and crash reports to PostHog under a random ID. Apple handles subscriptions, and RevenueCat keeps the record of them.
  • We don't sell your data, use it for advertising or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Apple Health data

With your permission, Ebbo reads the following from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • Heart rate variability (SDNN, and RMSSD where your Apple Watch records it)
  • Heart rate
  • Sleep
  • Steps

Ebbo uses this data only on your iPhone, to work out your morning score, your usual range, your trends and what moved your score. Ebbo doesn't send these readings, or anything calculated from them, to us or to anyone else. It doesn't use them for advertising or marketing.

You can change what Ebbo can read at any time. In the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Ebbo.

What stays on your iPhone

Ebbo stores these only on your iPhone, and deleting Ebbo deletes them:

  • your answers during setup, such as your wake-up time and how you've been feeling
  • your scores and history
  • your settings

Usage analytics and crash reports (PostHog)

Ebbo uses PostHog, Inc., which stores data in the United States, to see where people get stuck in the app and to fix crashes. The first time Ebbo opens, it creates a random ID. The ID isn't connected to your name, email address or Apple Account. PostHog stores the following under that ID:

  • The screens you open, and when. This includes the setup screens. Some setup screens appear only when Ebbo finds enough Apple Watch readings for a score, so the screens you saw can show whether it found enough. They never include the readings themselves.
  • Your answers to "How did you hear about Ebbo?" and "Does this look about right?" (only the answer).
  • When the subscription screen opens and closes, and when a trial or subscription starts.
  • Crash and error reports.
  • Device and app details: iPhone model, iOS version, app version, language, time zone, screen size, and whether the app came from the App Store or a test build.

Your IP address is used to connect to PostHog, but PostHog is set not to store it. Ebbo also asks PostHog not to work out your location from it.

Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details. Ebbo uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) to check whether you have a subscription. RevenueCat receives the following:

  • your purchase and subscription records from Apple
  • an anonymous ID that RevenueCat creates for the app
  • the identifier that Apple gives the app for your device
  • Ebbo's PostHog ID

With the PostHog ID, RevenueCat sends trial, renewal and cancellation events to PostHog. This shows us which parts of the app lead people to subscribe.

Notifications

Ebbo schedules all its reminders on your iPhone: the morning notice, the evening reminders to wear your Apple Watch and the reminder before a free trial ends. No notification server is involved. You can turn the reminders off in Settings, then Notifications, then Ebbo.

Links to other sites

Ebbo opens Apple's Terms of Use and the pages of published research papers in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. Ebbo doesn't ask for tracking permission and doesn't use the advertising identifier.
  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

How long data is kept

  • On your iPhone: until you delete Ebbo.
  • PostHog: analytics and crash reports are kept only while they help us improve Ebbo. We delete them sooner if you ask.
  • RevenueCat: purchase records are kept while they're needed to provide your subscription and to meet tax and accounting rules.

Your choices and rights

  • Health data: turn off any data type in the Health app, as described above. Ebbo then can't use it.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Settings.
  • Data on your iPhone: deleting Ebbo removes everything it stored.
  • Analytics and purchase records: to ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted, email mmcmahon1102@gmail.com. Ebbo has no account, so we can't tell which analytics came from you unless you subscribed. If you did, include the Order ID from your Apple receipt. It's in the email from Apple and at reportaproblem.apple.com, and it lets us find your RevenueCat record and the analytics linked to it. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, for example California or the United Kingdom, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it's used. Email us to use these rights.

Children

Ebbo isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
