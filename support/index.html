Common questions

When do I get my first score?

Wear your Apple Watch to bed. Ebbo gives your first score after 3 nights with at least 2 heart rate variability readings each night. Your usual range becomes fully your own after 7 nights.

Ebbo says it found no readings

  • Make sure Ebbo can read Apple Health. In the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Ebbo, and turn on every category.
  • Wear your Apple Watch to bed with sleep tracking on. You can set up a sleep schedule in the Health app, under Sleep.
  • Check that Wrist Detection is on. In the Watch app on your iPhone, go to Passcode.

Can I use Ebbo without an Apple Watch?

Ebbo needs an Apple Watch to give your own score. Without one, you can look around Ebbo with sample data, and every number from it is marked "Sample".

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Ebbo. You can also go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. To avoid being charged when a free trial ends, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

How do I get my subscription back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and open Ebbo. On the subscription screen, tap "Restore purchases".

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is Ebbo a medical device?

No. Ebbo is for general wellness. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you're worried about your health, talk to a doctor.

Where does my health data go?

Your health data stays on your iPhone. For the details, read the privacy policy.
